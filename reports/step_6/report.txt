To demonstrate the practical applicability of our SER system, we integrated the trained CNN–BiGRU model into a small Python web service implemented with FastAPI. The service exposes a /predict endpoint that accepts an uploaded audio file, performs the same preprocessing steps as during training (resampling, log-Mel spectrogram computation and padding/truncation), and returns the predicted emotion together with the posterior probabilities over the four classes. A Flutter mobile client records short speech samples on the device, sends them to the Python server via HTTP and displays the predicted emotion label in real time.
